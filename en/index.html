MutelinkTalk in VRChat with a voice you like, without sharing your own.

A VRChat conversation tool for people who prefer not to speak (Windows; free app)

Download
Getting started
BOOTH (supporter edition)

Turn speech into text

Enter text with speech recognition (on your PC or in the cloud), your keyboard or the VR keyboard.

Send voice and chatbox messages

Read text with VOICEVOX, Irodori-TTS or Qwen3-TTS (10 languages), and send it to the VRChat chatbox.

Translate before sending

Speak Japanese and send a translation in English or another language. Translation can run locally or through Azure.

Read what others say

Recognize VRChat audio and display what others say as text, with translations.

Use it in VR

Use a VR keyboard, controller hotkeys and panels placed on your hand or in your view.

Use your own voice too

Pass your microphone voice through with one button.

About this site ​

Press “?” at the top right of the app settings to see explanations beside each setting. This site covers material that is harder to present inside the app.

  • Before installing: Getting started, Supporter edition, Privacy
  • Compare your options: Voice samples, Speech recognition engines, Translation engines
  • Longer setup guides: Set up Azure Translator
  • If something goes wrong: Troubleshooting, Q&A

Requirements ​

  • Windows 10/11
  • VRChat
  • VB-Audio Virtual Cable (free): needed to route synthesized speech into the VRChat microphone. Not needed for chatbox-only use.
  • Vulkan-compatible GPU (optional): needed for Irodori-TTS and Qwen3-TTS. VOICEVOX runs without a GPU.
  • SteamVR (optional): needed only for VR panels and controller operation.

Tested hardware ​

The developer tests on the following hardware. Operation on lower-spec systems is not guaranteed.

  • OS: Windows 11
  • GPU: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070
  • CPU: AMD Ryzen 7 7700
  • Memory: 32GB (DDR5)
  • Headset: Meta Quest 3 (PCVR)
