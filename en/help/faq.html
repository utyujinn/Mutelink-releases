Q&A ​

Common questions. For problems, see Troubleshooting.

Installation and updates ​

Windows says it protected my PC ​

This can appear because the app is not code-signed. Click More info, then Run anyway. No administrator privileges are needed.

How do I know about new versions? ​

The app notifies you at startup. You can also check in Settings > Other > About.

Where are setting explanations? ​

Press “?” at the top right of settings to show explanations beside each option. This site does not list every setting.

Why are so few settings visible? ​

Settings > Usage shows only options relevant to your chosen uses. Choose Show all on the same page to see everything.

Speech recognition ​

Which recognition engine should I choose? ​

For Japanese, try Parakeet Japanese TDT or Haas-ja; English, Parakeet English; others, Qwen3-ASR or Whisper turbo. See engine comparisons.

I want my audio to stay on my PC ​

Local Parakeet, Haas-ja, Qwen3-ASR and Whisper turbo keep audio on your PC. Cloud Web Speech/Google send it to Microsoft/Google (Privacy).

Friends’ names are recognized incorrectly ​

For Parakeet Japanese TDT, registering words under Settings > Speaking > Language and recognition > Words and names makes them more likely to appear.

I cannot select Auto language ​

Auto is available only for Qwen3-ASR and Whisper turbo.

Voices ​

Which voice should I choose? ​

Choose VOICEVOX for character voices or no GPU, Irodori-TTS for fast natural Japanese, and Qwen3-TTS for other languages. Listen to voice samples.

Do I need a GPU? ​

VOICEVOX and chatbox-only use do not need a GPU. Irodori-TTS and Qwen3-TTS need a Vulkan-compatible GPU.

Can it speak in a voice like mine? ​

Register a recording with Irodori-TTS or Qwen3-TTS (supporter feature). Use only your own voice or one whose owner has explicitly consented.

Can it read languages other than Japanese? ​

Qwen3-TTS reads English, Chinese, Korean, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese and Russian.

Translation ​

Is translation free? ​

Translation, translated chatbox messages and incoming-speech translation are free. Only reading translated text aloud requires the supporter edition.

Which translation engine is best? ​

It depends on the language. Read your language’s outputs in translation comparisons. Choose PLaMo or TranslateGemma to keep text on your PC.

Can I review a translation before sending? ​

No. Translation happens just before sending and is sent immediately.

Incoming speech ​

What is incoming speech? ​

Mutelink transcribes VRChat audio to display other people’s words. Translations can also be displayed.

Can others see incoming-speech text? ​

The main-window list and VR panels are private to you. If you enable chatbox output (off by default), everyone in the instance can see it.

VR ​

Is SteamVR required? ​

No. Use it on desktop without SteamVR, including keyboard shortcuts for sending and language switching (Settings > Other > Hotkeys).

Can it start with SteamVR? ​

Yes. Enable Start/exit with SteamVR under Settings > Appearance > VR. Registration happens when connected, so start SteamVR before Mutelink the first time.

Supporter edition and data ​

Must I buy the supporter edition? ​

No. The app is free without a purchase. See supporter features for the differences.

What data leaves my PC? ​

See Privacy.
